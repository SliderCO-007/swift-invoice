SCANGO INVOICE - MANUAL ONBOARDING EMAIL TEMPLATE
================================================================================
Use this template for manual outreach to users who signed up but haven't yet 
set up their company info or created an invoice. Plain-text, highly personal 
outreach from a personal email address has a much higher response and conversion 
rate compared to automated, styled HTML emails.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUBJECT LINE OPTIONS (Pick one):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Option 1 (Highly recommended - personal & high open-rate):
Subject: quick question about ScanGo

Option 2 (Help & support focused):
Subject: Help setting up your ScanGo Invoice profile?

Option 3 (Action-oriented):
Subject: Getting your first invoice sent on ScanGo Invoice

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EMAIL BODY:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Hi [User First Name],

I wanted to personally reach out and welcome you to ScanGo Invoice. 

I noticed you signed up recently but haven't had a chance to get your business profile set up or create your first invoice yet. Completely understand how busy things get, so I wanted to make it as easy as possible to get you up and running today.

If you have 3 minutes, here is the quick-start checklist to get your first professional invoice sent:

1. Add your company details (1 minute)
   This fills in your logo, contact info, and tax details so they display beautifully on your invoices.
   👉 Set up company profile: https://scangoinvoice.com/settings

2. Generate your first invoice (2 minutes)
   Enter your client details and items, then generate a beautiful, clean PDF or payment link.
   👉 Create first invoice: https://scangoinvoice.com/invoice/new

Once those are set up, you'll be able to send professional invoices in seconds and even accept secure credit card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or ACH payments directly online.

By the way, if you hit a snag or if ScanGo wasn't quite what you were looking for, would you mind letting me know what we could do better? (Just hit reply—I read every email!)

Best,

[Your Name]
Founder, ScanGo Invoice
support@scangoinvoice.com

================================================================================
TIPS FOR SENDING:
================================================================================
1. Send this from your personal business email (e.g., curtis@scangoinvoice.com) 
   rather than a generic "noreply@" or "support@" address.
2. Replace [User First Name] with their actual name. If they didn't provide 
   one, use "there" (e.g., "Hi there,").
3. Keep the styling clean and strictly plain text. Aggressive bolding, massive 
   fonts, or huge images will trigger spam filters and look automated.
