> **Target Audience:** General contractors, plumbers, electricians, landscapers, freelancers, and independent service business owners.

---

## 💡 The Core Marketing Psychology

Most invoicing apps only advertise to one side of the transaction: the business owner's bookkeeping. 

**The real bottleneck in cash flow is the client's payment friction.**
- When paying an invoice requires downloading a PDF, opening desktop banking, typing in account/routing numbers, or writing a check, the client flags it as *"deal with this later"*.
- The invoice sits unpaid for 20 to 30 days—not because the client lacks the money, but because the process is a chore.
- The business owner then has to spend Sunday nights following up with awkward reminder texts.

**ScanGo Invoice solves both sides simultaneously:**
1. **For the Business Owner:** 60 seconds from job completion to sent invoice directly from the phone. No crumpled receipts, no manual data entry, no lost weekend hours.
2. **For the Client:** 5 seconds to pay. Point the phone camera at the QR code, tap Apple Pay or Google Pay, and it's settled. No app download, no account creation, zero friction.
