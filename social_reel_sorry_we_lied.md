> **Concept:** Pattern-interrupt hook leveraging curiosity & confession psychology to showcase that ScanGo Invoice is a complete business operations and payment tool, not just a static invoice generator.

---

## 📌 Campaign Overview
- **Primary Hook:** *"Sorry, we lied"*
- **Target Audience:** Freelancers, contractors, field service providers, agency owners, small business founders.
- **Key Value Props:** 
  1. Real-time project hour & expense/receipt tracking.
  2. 1-Click conversion from tracked projects directly into pre-filled invoices.
  3. Dynamic Stripe Scan-to-Pay QR codes & instant credit card/Apple Pay/ACH checkout.
  4. 6 designer templates with brand customizer.
  5. Multi-seat team collaboration with hidden billing rates for crew members.

### Production & Editing Notes (Script 2)
- **Speed Multiplier:** Speed up raw phone screen captures to **1.25x – 1.35x** so form taps and transitions feel snappy and instant.
- **Audio FX:** 
  - *Woosh* on transitions.
  - *Camera shutter* sound when snapping the receipt.
  - *Payment 'Ding'* / *Cash Register* sound when zooming on the QR code.
- **Captions:** Use high-contrast font (e.g., Bold Montserrat or Proxima Nova with black outline/pill background) centered in the safe zone.
