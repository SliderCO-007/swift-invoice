## ✂️ CapCut / Video Editor Assembly Recipe

1. **Clip Speed:** Set all screen recordings to **1.25x – 1.35x speed** so UI interactions feel snappy.
2. **Text Overlays:**
   - Word highlight style (e.g., yellow `#facc15` or cyan `#00d2ff` on active words).
   - Place text in center-screen safe area (avoid bottom 20% where TikTok/Reels captions sit).
3. **Sound Effects (SFX):**
   - **0:00:** Sub bass drop or record-scratch.
   - **0:08:** Camera shutter click on receipt upload.
   - **0:13:** Digital "whoosh" or "pop" on 1-click convert.
   - **0:18:** Cash register ding (`cha-ching`) on QR code zoom.
4. **Background Music:**
   - Low-volume upbeat lofi hip-hop or rhythmic tech synth (duck volume to ~15% under voiceover).
