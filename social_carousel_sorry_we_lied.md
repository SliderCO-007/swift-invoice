## 🎯 Why Facebook Looked Odd & How This Solves It
1. **The 4:5 Aspect Ratio Issue:** Instagram natively supports tall 4:5 cards (`1080x1350`). However, **Facebook's feed container strictly defaults to 1:1 Square (`1080x1080`)**. When 4:5 images are uploaded to Facebook, Facebook either auto-crops the top/bottom text or adds dark letterboxing.
2. **The 1:1 Square Mode:** The carousel studio now includes a dedicated **Facebook 1:1 Square mode** with adjusted padding, centered typography, and compact feature cards so every single word and badge stays in Facebook's safe zone.
3. **Direct Links in Copy:** Unlike Instagram (which forces "link in bio"), Facebook post text supports direct clickable URLs. The Facebook copy includes your direct app URL (`https://swift-invoice-7a760.web.app`).
